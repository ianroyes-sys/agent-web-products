## READ THIS BEFORE SENDING ANYTHING

### CAN-SPAM requirements — blocking

Every commercial email below is written to comply with 15 U.S.C. §7704. Four requirements are non-negotiable and one of them is **not satisfied today**:

| Requirement | Status |
|---|---|
| Accurate From, Reply-To, and routing headers | Satisfied — send from a real personal address on a real domain |
| Non-deceptive subject line | Satisfied — every subject below describes the actual contents |
| Clear opt-out mechanism, honored within 10 business days | Satisfied — the opt-out line is in every signature block below |
| **A valid physical postal address of the sender** | **NOT SATISFIED** |

**Do not run any bulk or sequenced send until a physical mailing address is in place.** A PO box or a commercial mail-receiving agency address is acceptable under the statute; a home address is legal but rarely advisable. This should be resolved in the same errand as the LLC filing.

Every signature block below contains the token `[PHYSICAL MAILING ADDRESS]`. **If that token is still present, the email is not sendable.** Treat it as a hard gate.

### Additional posture

- **One-to-one manual sends are exempt from most of this in practice but not in law.** The safe rule: include the address and opt-out on every commercial email, including the first manual one. It costs two lines.
- **No misleading urgency.** The dates in these emails are real contract expiry dates from federal records. Never invent a deadline, never imply a limited-time offer that is not limited, never fabricate scarcity.
- **Every number in these emails is traceable** to `data/out/*.json`, generated 2026-08-16 from USAspending.gov. If a recipient asks for the source, send the USAspending link for the specific award. That willingness to be checked is the whole credibility play.
- **Data staleness.** These figures are a 2026-08-16 snapshot. Day counts ("expires in 22 days") drift. Regenerate before a send, or use absolute dates only. **Prefer absolute dates in email.**
- **Suppression list.** Maintain one from day one. Anyone who opts out, anywhere, never gets contacted again from any RMG product.
